# Equity Research Review Policy

This is a fictional but realistic working policy for the annual-report corpus
used in this assignment. It is included as a shareable and safely editable
source document.

## Review cadence

The research team performs a full portfolio review every 90 days. Material
company announcements are reviewed within two business days rather than waiting
for the next scheduled review.

## Source requirements

Each company assessment must cite the latest available annual report and must
distinguish reported facts from analyst interpretation. Apple, Microsoft, and
Tesla are the three companies covered by the current demonstration corpus.

## Escalation threshold

A holding is escalated for an additional risk review when its internal risk
score reaches 70 out of 100. The reviewer records the evidence, the source file,
and the date of the decision.

## Retention

Superseded working notes are removed from the active retrieval corpus after the
replacement has been approved. They may remain in a separately controlled
archive, but the production assistant must not retrieve them as current policy.
